# PA35 acceptance gates (read by scripts/check.pl; see README.md), in three
# groups checked in order: correctness, then compiler speed, then codegen.
#
# A gate with `from` and `step` also has a progress ladder (scripts/progress.pl,
# `make -C pa35 test-progress`): rungs at limit x (1 + step)^k up to `from`;
# a measurement passes every rung at or above it, so each rung is one step of
# measured progress toward the limit and the last rung is the gate itself.
# Steps follow measurement noise: 0.5% for the workload's instruction and
# cache-simulation counts, 1% for the kernels and text size, 2% for memory,
# 5% for timed runs.
