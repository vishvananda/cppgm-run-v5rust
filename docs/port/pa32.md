## Owners and mechanism

Consulted the handout, contract, spec, conversion plan and frozen
`native/select/{analysis,emit_values,emit_calls,emit_control,mir_cleanup}.cpp`,
operand roles, object-body retirement and frame-liveness consumers.
Detailed ownership is maintained in [source-map.tsv](source-map.tsv).

- `scalar_selection.rs`, `integer_facts.rs` and `scalar_mir.rs` consume recorded
  nonnegative capacities and `nowrap`, select frozen u32 arithmetic/shift widths,
  and truncate large multiply immediates with exact low/high MIR words. Typed
  shift widths reach views, flags checks and encoding. O0 forms are preserved.
- `call_selection.rs` uses the durable `Program::function_defined` fact after
  an encoded callee body is released. Weak/replaceable results keep their
  normalization. An ownership regression compares caller MIR before/after release.
- `address_facts.rs` distinguishes call-only value uses, rematerializes admitted
  frame/direct-symbol/register addresses, and recomposes expressions after an
  incompatible index permanently makes a base eager. Argument consumers stage
  addresses in parallel; homed bases form after moves in their final ABI register,
  including indirect-target protection and the original stack-constant order.
- `home_forwarding.rs` translates available frame-home holders: predecessor meets,
  handler/landing cuts, width/volatile/call/alias barriers, extending reloads and
  scratch-reader substitution. It runs after return-slot forwarding and before
  LEA cleanup and frame-store liveness. Tests cover joins and narrow/volatile loads.

Architecture audit: typed LowIR and one function's MIR/scratch; compact identities,
small copied descriptors and dense holder states, with no frontend retention or
text reparsing. Eager-base recomposition is monotone. Holder states fall from
unknown to one home to none, skipping unchanged inputs. Scratch ends with its
function. Existing allocator, emission order and optimizer budgets remain intact;
no unsafe code, dependency, phase environment read or implementation delegation.
